Standard licence texts for `scripts/licences.mjs`. They are used only for a component that ships no
licence file of its own; `{copyright}` is filled from the component's authors (or the copyright
line in `scripts/licences-native.json`). Sources: Apache-2.0 from `serde`, MPL-2.0 from `cssparser`,
Unlicense from `memchr`, Zlib from `bytemuck`, BSD-3-Clause from `alloc-no-stdlib` (each with its
copyright line replaced), MIT and ISC as published by the SPDX licence list.
