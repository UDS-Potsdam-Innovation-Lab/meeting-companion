Settings panel (requested by the human): one place for the app settings, starting with audio.

- Audio: choose the microphone (input device) instead of always the system default, live input level meter and a short test, input gain / noise handling if the engine allows, what happens when the device disappears.
- Local / on-device: speech model (the picker from 003 moves here: found models, where they were found, language, size), speaker model (004), where meetings and transcripts are stored, how long data is kept, the default language of meetings.
- Other settings: app language (de/en), theme (light/dark/system), and room for later sections (language model engine, export connectors, AI voice).
- Settings are stored locally (encrypted like the meeting data where they are sensitive), apply immediately, and are reachable from the top bar and the keyboard. Responsive like the rest of the app.
